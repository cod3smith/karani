>
  Nine job boards, thousands of postings a day, and maybe five of them are
  a role you would take at a company that would pay you properly. Finding
  those five takes forty-five minutes a day and still misses most of them.

  karani is a clerk for that problem. It reads every posting, drops the
  ones that fail your rules before any model is called, scores what
  survives against your actual resume, drafts the whole application —
  and **hands you the pen**. You press submit. It never does.
</Hero>

<Band label="What it does with a posting" title="Two tiers, and only one of them costs money">

The first tier is arithmetic. Role family, seniority, geography, remote
status, comp anchored to a currency keyword, skill overlap against your
profile — all word-boundary matched, all deterministic, no model involved.
About 95% of a pass ends here, for free.

<RunReport note="karani run">{samples.transcripts.hunt}</RunReport>

That split is the whole architecture ([ADR
0001](/decisions/0001-tiered-filter-not-single-llm-pass/)). Running a model
over every RemoteOK posting would be expensive and no better; running one
over the two dozen that survived is cheap enough to be worth doing
carefully.

</Band>

<Band label="What a model is for" title="A verdict you can argue with">

The second tier reads the survivors against your resume and comes back
with a score, a three-way verdict, and claims about you that each carry
the line of your own resume behind them.

<Verdict note="karani qualify">{samples.verdicts.qualified}</Verdict>

Every strength has a quote. That is not a prompt asking nicely: a claim
whose citation karani cannot verify in your own records is **dropped, not
repaired** ([CLAUDE.md
4.13](https://github.com/cod3smith/karani/blob/main/CLAUDE.md)), because a
fabricated story gets probed for ten minutes by someone who interviews for
a living.

The disagreements are the useful part. When karani says skip and you would
apply, tell it — `karani verdict 1544 apply` — and that pair becomes a
few-shot example in the next qualify prompt. Its taste converges on yours
with no retraining.

</Band>

<Band label="The last mile" title="It fills the form in. You send it.">

`karani console` writes one self-contained HTML file: every pack waiting to
be sent, each with the apply link, the rendered documents, and a
field-by-field answer sheet with a copy button on every field. No server,
no port, no process — it opens from iCloud on a phone, which is where a
queue actually gets cleared.

<RunReport note="karani console">{samples.transcripts.console}</RunReport>

The sheet answers that job's **real** questions. Greenhouse, Lever and
Ashby publish their application forms on the same public endpoints karani
ingests from, so the wording and the option values are the company's own
rather than a guess at a standard form.

And every answer says where it came from. Work authorization, sponsorship,
relocation and start date are resolved from legal facts **you declare and
sign**, by code, never by a model — the same question has opposite correct
answers depending on the role's shape, so "the model usually gets it
right" is not a defence ([ADR
0023](/decisions/0023-submission-console-and-write-back/)). Demographic
questions are left blank. Anything karani has no source for is flagged
rather than invented.

</Band>

<Band label="What it costs" title="Billed work degrades. It never halts, and never overspends.">

The reference configuration runs bulk qualification on a local model at
zero token cost, and keeps a hosted model for drafting — the low-volume
step where quality is what gets the interview.

Past the cap, or with no API key set, a billed provider is swapped for the
local model and the pass says once that it happened. A missing key is never
a hard failure and a spent budget never stops the hunt ([ADR
0019](/decisions/0019-hosted-tiers-behind-a-budget/)).

</Band>

<Band label="What leaves your machine" title="As little as you configure">

karani is self-hosted and every integration is opt-in. Your resume, the
database and every generated pack stay on disk.

| Destination | What goes there |
|---|---|
| Job boards | Nothing about you. Public endpoints, read only. |
| LLM provider | The posting, your resume, your positioning, past verdicts as examples. Set `provider = "local"` and nothing leaves. |
| Slack, Notion, object storage | Shortlists, packs and application state — only if you configure them. |
| Gmail | Nothing outbound. The watcher holds a **read-only** scope and cannot send, delete or mark read. |

API keys live in `.env` and never in `karani.toml`, which the docs build
checks: a config field whose name looks like a credential fails it.

</Band>

<Band label="Where it stands" title="One user, and it is his own job hunt">

karani is on [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/karani/) as `karani`. It is a
personal tool rather than a product: it is built for one person's hunt, it
makes no multi-tenant assumptions, and there is no hosted version and no
plan for one. If you want to use it, fork it and rewrite `karani.toml` and
`data/resume.md` — [the vision page](/project/vision/) says what that
involves and what karani deliberately will not do.

The one thing it will never do is press submit for you.

</Band>
