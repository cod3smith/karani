## What the licence does not cover

**The services karani talks to have their own terms.** Job boards, ATS
APIs, model providers, Slack, Notion, Google. karani reads public endpoints
and holds a read-only mail scope, but how you use it is between you and
them.

**Postings are not karani's to license.** Job descriptions belong to the
companies that wrote them. karani stores them locally so it can filter and
qualify them; that is not a licence to republish them.

**What you send is yours.** karani drafts; you sign and submit. Every claim
in a pack traces back to a record you wrote, and the attestations on an
application form are derived from facts you declared — because they are
attestations you are accountable for, not karani ([ADR
0023](/decisions/0023-submission-console-and-write-back/)).

## Examples on this site

Every posting, company, person and transcript in these pages is
**fabricated**. The candidate is Ada Mwangi, who does not exist. The
companies are Northwind Robotics, Helix Data, Meridian Labs and Calderwood
Health, none of which exists either, and the application URLs on their
postings resolve to nothing.

That is a build gate rather than a promise: the fabricated names live in one
module, and a check over the built output fails if anything from a real
resume reaches a page. The repository is public and so are these docs, so
there was no version of this worth doing by hand.
